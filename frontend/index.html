Questions

Do I need to know accounting?

No. You only need your monthly sales and expenses. The agents explain everything in plain words.

How do I pay?

Pay with JazzCash, Easypaisa or a bank transfer, then enter your transaction ID. Your plan is activated once the payment is verified.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are monthly with no contract. When a paid month ends, you move back to Free.

Is my data private?

Your sales data is only used to create your own reports and is never shown to other users.
